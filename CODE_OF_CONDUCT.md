# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

FogWS should be a respectful, focused and technically honest project space.
Contributors and users must be able to discuss bugs, protocol behavior, design
trade-offs, security, performance, tests and roadmap decisions without personal
hostility or gatekeeping.

## Engineering Culture

Project spaces are for engineering work. Technical discussion may be direct and
demanding: maintainers may challenge designs, reject patches, require tests and
question performance or compatibility claims. Keep that criticism tied to the
work and be hard on the problem, not on the person.

AI tools may assist engineering, but the person submitting a contribution owns
the code, reasoning, tests and consequences. Don't submit changes that you
cannot explain, debug and maintain.

Political, religious, ideological and other unrelated debates are outside the
scope of FogWS project spaces.

## Expected Behavior

- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on behavior, code, design, tests or documentation.
- Ask before assuming bad intent.
- Acknowledge and correct mistakes.
- Protect security-sensitive and private information.
- Give credit for ideas, reports and contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory, sexualized or demeaning language or behavior.
- Personal attacks, threats or humiliation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing private vulnerability details before coordinated disclosure.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.

## Scope And Enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, security
reports and other spaces where someone represents FogWS.

Maintainers may ask participants to stop specific behavior, edit or remove
comments, close or lock discussions, decline contributions, block participants
or escalate to platform moderation. Actions should be proportionate to impact
and repeated behavior.

## Reporting

- Maintainer: [GefMar](https://github.com/GefMar)
- Public non-sensitive concerns: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/AmberFog/fogws/discussions)

If private handling is required, use the public channel only to request a
private coordination path. Don't publish personal data, private screenshots,
credentials or security details.
